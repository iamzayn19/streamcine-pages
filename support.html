When Contacting Support

If possible, include:

  • Your HulaMov app version and build number.
  • Your macOS version.
  • The screen or provider you were using.
  • A brief description of the problem.
  • A screenshot when useful.
Never send passwords, Apple Account credentials, Netflix/Hulu/Peacock passwords, API keys, cryptographic private keys or other sensitive secrets.

Subscriptions

Use HulaMov → Settings → Subscription to restore purchases, change your HulaMov Pro plan or open Apple's subscription-management controls.

Subscription billing, cancellation and renewal are handled by Apple.

Provider Pages

Netflix, Hulu and Peacock control their own login, playback, DRM, account and regional restrictions.

If a provider prevents a particular action inside WebKit, HulaMov cannot bypass that restriction.

Privacy & Local Data

Use:

HulaMov → Settings → Data → Delete All Local Data

to remove HulaMov-owned local data from your Mac.

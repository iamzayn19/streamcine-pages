StreamCine Support Need help with StreamCine for macOS? Contact Email: iamzayn19@gmail.com When Contacting Support If possible, include: • Your StreamCine version and build number. • Your macOS version. • The screen or provider you were using. • A brief description of the problem. • A screenshot when useful. Never send passwords, Apple Account credentials, Netflix/Hulu/Peacock passwords, API keys, cryptographic private keys, or other sensitive secrets. Subscriptions Use: StreamCine → Settings → Subscription to: • Restore purchases. • Change your StreamCine Pro plan. • Open Apple's subscription-management controls. Subscription billing, cancellation, and renewal are handled by Apple. Provider Pages Netflix, Hulu, and Peacock control their own login, playback, DRM, account, and regional restrictions. If a provider prevents a particular action inside WebKit, StreamCine cannot bypass that restriction. Privacy and Local Data Use: StreamCine → Settings → Data → Delete All Local Data to remove StreamCine-owned local data from your Mac. For server-side privacy requests, contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL

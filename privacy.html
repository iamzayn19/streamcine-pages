StreamCine Privacy Policy Effective Date: September 2, 2026 StreamCine respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the StreamCine macOS application and its supporting backend service. 1. About StreamCine StreamCine is a movie and television discovery application. It allows users to search and browse entertainment content, view ratings and metadata, discover streaming availability, and open supported streaming provider pages. StreamCine does not require you to create a personal StreamCine account using your name or email address. StreamCine does not host or redistribute Netflix, Hulu, or Peacock video streams. 2. Information StreamCine Processes To operate and secure the service, StreamCine may process: • An anonymous installation identifier. • Cryptographic public-key and request-signing information used to authenticate the application securely. • Authentication tokens, timestamps, nonces, and related security information. • Search queries, selected filters, provider selections, and media identifiers submitted to provide requested results. • StreamCine Pro entitlement information provided through Apple StoreKit. • Limited operational and security information necessary to diagnose errors, protect the backend, enforce service limits, and maintain reliability. StreamCine does not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. 3. Information Stored Locally on Your Mac Depending on the features you use, StreamCine may store the following locally on your Mac: • Cryptographic installation and authentication information in macOS Keychain. • StreamCine preferences and appearance settings. • Free-search usage and feature state. • Watch History, when enabled. • Image and network caches. • Temporary application data. • WebKit website data, cookies, and local website storage created when Netflix, Hulu, or Peacock pages are opened inside StreamCine. 4. Streaming Provider Websites When you select a Netflix, Hulu, or Peacock watch option, StreamCine may display the provider's website inside an in-app WebKit browser. Information you enter into those websites, including provider usernames, passwords, billing information, or other account information, is handled by the provider and WebKit according to that provider's own privacy policy and terms. StreamCine does not intentionally collect your Netflix, Hulu, or Peacock password. 5. Backend and Data Providers StreamCine uses third-party infrastructure and data services to provide app functionality. These may include: • Firebase / Google Cloud • MDBList • Streaming Availability API by Movie of the Night • ReelDB • Apple StoreKit and App Store services Only information reasonably necessary to perform the requested feature is transmitted. Third-party services are also governed by their own privacy policies and terms. 6. Data Retention Most user-facing StreamCine state is stored locally on your Mac. Backend security and operational records may be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and protect the StreamCine service, or as required by applicable law. StreamCine does not maintain a traditional personal profile containing your name, email address, or streaming-provider password. 7. Delete All Local Data StreamCine provides a "Delete All Local Data" option in Settings. When confirmed, this removes StreamCine-owned local data from that Mac, including applicable: • Watch History • Local preferences • Caches • Local authentication and installation data • StreamCine Keychain data • Provider website data stored through StreamCine's WebKit browser Deleting local data may sign you out of Netflix, Hulu, or Peacock inside StreamCine. Deleting local data does NOT: • Cancel your StreamCine Pro subscription. • Delete Apple's App Store purchase history. • Delete your Netflix, Hulu, or Peacock account. • Delete information independently held by Apple or another third party. 8. Privacy and Deletion Requests If you have a privacy question or believe StreamCine retains server-side information associated with your installation and would like to request deletion, contact StreamCine Support. 9. Security StreamCine uses HTTPS, short-lived authentication tokens, signed backend requests, Keychain storage, and other reasonable safeguards designed to protect the service. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security. 10. Children StreamCine is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Movies, television programs, and third-party providers may have their own age ratings and parental-control requirements. 11. Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when StreamCine's features, data practices, providers, or applicable requirements change. The Effective Date displayed above will be updated when changes are published. 12. Contact For privacy questions or requests: SUPPORT_EMAIL

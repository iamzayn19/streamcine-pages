Privacy Policy

How HulaMov handles information when you use the macOS application and its supporting backend.

Effective date: September 3, 2026

1. About HulaMov

HulaMov is a movie and television discovery application. It allows users to search and browse entertainment content, view ratings and metadata, discover streaming availability, and open supported streaming-provider pages.

HulaMov does not require you to create a personal HulaMov account using your name or email address. HulaMov does not host or redistribute Netflix, Hulu, or Peacock video streams.

2. Information HulaMov Processes

To operate and secure the service, HulaMov may process:

  • An anonymous installation identifier.
  • Cryptographic public-key and request-signing information used to authenticate the application.
  • Authentication tokens, timestamps, nonces and related security information.
  • Search queries, selected filters, provider selections and media identifiers.
  • HulaMov Pro entitlement information provided through Apple StoreKit.
  • Limited operational and security information necessary to diagnose errors, protect the backend and maintain service reliability.

HulaMov does not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Mac

Depending on the features you use, HulaMov may store:

  • Cryptographic installation and authentication information in macOS Keychain.
  • HulaMov preferences and appearance settings.
  • Free-search usage and feature state.
  • Watch History, when enabled.
  • Image and network caches.
  • Temporary application data.
  • WebKit website data, cookies and local website storage created when Netflix, Hulu or Peacock pages are opened inside HulaMov.

4. Streaming Provider Websites

When you select a Netflix, Hulu or Peacock watch option, HulaMov may display the provider's website inside an in-app WebKit browser.

Information you enter into those websites, including provider login or billing information, is handled by that provider according to its own privacy policy and terms.

HulaMov does not intentionally collect your Netflix, Hulu or Peacock password.

5. Backend and Data Providers

HulaMov uses third-party infrastructure and data services which may include:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud
  • MDBList
  • Streaming Availability API by Movie of the Night
  • ReelDB
  • Apple StoreKit and App Store services

Only information reasonably necessary to perform the requested application feature is transmitted.

6. Data Retention

Most user-facing HulaMov state is stored locally on your Mac.

Backend security and operational records may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot and protect the service, or where required by applicable law.

7. Delete All Local Data

HulaMov provides a Delete All Local Data option in Settings.

When confirmed, this may remove HulaMov-owned:

  • Watch History
  • Preferences
  • Caches
  • Authentication and installation data
  • HulaMov Keychain data
  • Provider website data stored through HulaMov's WebKit browser
Deleting local data may sign you out of Netflix, Hulu or Peacock inside HulaMov.

It does not cancel your HulaMov Pro subscription, delete Apple's App Store purchase history, or delete your Netflix, Hulu or Peacock account.

9. Security

HulaMov uses HTTPS, short-lived authentication tokens, signed backend requests, Keychain storage and other reasonable safeguards.

10. Children

HulaMov is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

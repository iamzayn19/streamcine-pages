StreamCine Data Sources & Attributions StreamCine combines information from multiple sources to provide movie and television discovery, ratings, artwork, cast information, awards information, and streaming-provider availability. MDBList MDBList is used by the StreamCine backend for movie and television metadata, ratings, discovery feeds, catalog information, and media enrichment where available. https://mdblist.com/ Streaming Availability API by Movie of the Night Streaming availability information is provided by Streaming Availability API by Movie of the Night. This service is used for supported streaming-provider availability, provider catalog information, and genuine provider destination links where available. https://www.movieofthenight.com/about/api ReelDB ReelDB may be used for cast/person enrichment and actor profile imagery where available and where permitted by applicable source terms. https://reeldb.io/ Apple Apple StoreKit and App Store services are used to offer, verify, restore, and manage StreamCine Pro subscriptions. Apple controls App Store billing and purchase records. https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/ Firebase / Google Cloud StreamCine uses Firebase and Google Cloud infrastructure for backend API functionality, application configuration, caching, security, and service operation. Netflix, Hulu, and Peacock StreamCine may display genuine provider destination pages for Netflix, Hulu, and Peacock inside an in-app WebKit browser. Provider accounts, subscriptions, website data, cookies, content availability, playback, and DRM are controlled by the applicable provider.
